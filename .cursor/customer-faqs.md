# Pay By Card & Rewards – Customer FAQs

Answers to common questions about Pay By Card and Prospa Reward Points. You can make payments and manage your account in the [Prospa App](https://www.prospa.com/) or [Customer portal](https://app.prospa.com/).

---

## Pay By Card

1. **What is Pay By Card?**
   Pay By Card lets you pay any biller — think the ATO or your suppliers — with your Visa, Mastercard or Amex, even if they don't accept cards directly. Turn on Prospa Rewards and you'll also earn Reward Points on eligible payments.

2. **What are the benefits of paying bills with a card through Prospa?**
   Pay By Card lets you use your card to pay almost any biller, even those that don't accept cards. That means you can lean on your card's credit terms to free up cash flow, earn Reward Points on eligible payments, and keep earning your card's own points at the same time. The Platform fee may also be tax deductible — chat to your accountant.

3. **Which cards can I use?**
   You can use Australian‑issued Visa, Mastercard or Amex debit or credit cards from any provider, not just Prospa. We don't accept international cards or bank transfers. Fees depend on your card type.

4. **What are the fees for Pay By Card?**
   Our **Platform fee** includes a **Service fee** (Visa and Mastercard 1.2%, Amex 2.1%, ex GST) and, if you opt in to earn Reward Points, a **Reward fee** of 1% (+ GST) on that payment. You'll see the full breakdown before you confirm the payment. Fees are calculated on the payment amount.

5. **When am I charged?**
   We charge the full amount (bill + fees + GST) to your card when you submit the payment. The breakdown is shown before you confirm.

6. **When does the biller receive the payment?**
   Your card is charged straight away when you submit a payment and we start processing it. Funds typically reach your biller within **2 business days**. Payments may show as **Processing** in your transaction history until the transfer completes, then update to **Paid** (or **Declined** if the payment can't be completed).

7. **Are fees tax‑deductible?**
   Depending on your circumstances, our Platform fee may be tax deductible. This is not financial advice — please consult your accountant.

8. **Is there a minimum or maximum payment?**
   There's no minimum or maximum payment set by us. Your card limits still apply.

9. **What will the biller see on their end?**
   After processing, the payment will appear on the billers with your provided reference.

10. **Can I pay my BAS via Pay By Card?**
    Yes. You can pay your BAS and other ATO‑related payments using Pay By Card, while earning Reward Points (if Prospa Rewards is turned on) and freeing up your cash flow.

## Earning Prospa Reward Points

1. **What are Reward Points?**
   Reward Points are earned through Prospa Rewards — our loyalty program. Earn points on eligible activity like Pay By Card payments, then convert them to Qantas Points or redeem them for gift cards and other rewards in the Prospa App or customer portal.

2. **How do I earn Reward Points?**
   You can earn Prospa Reward points on eligible activities like Pay By Card payments. Turn on Prospa Rewards for each payment and earn **1 Reward Point per $1** of eligible spend — on top of your card's own points. A **1% + GST Reward fee** applies when you opt in. More ways to earn Reward Points are coming soon.

3. **What can I use Prospa Reward Points for?**
   You can convert Reward Points to Qantas Points via Qantas Business Rewards, or redeem them for gift cards and other rewards — all from the Prospa App or customer portal.

4. **What is the Reward fee?**
   When you opt in to earn points on a payment, we charge a **1% Reward fee (+ GST)** on total transaction amount. We'll show you the fee and the points you'll earn before you confirm.

5. **Do I earn points on every payment?**
   No. Only eligible transactions or activities with Prospa Rewards turned on earn points. With Pay By Card, that's **1 Reward Point per full $1 of eligible value**. Some payment types or billers are excluded, the amount is rounded down and GST is excluded.

6. **Can I earn points on bank transfers?**
   No. Pay By Card is card‑only (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and doesn't support bank transfers.

7. **Are there bonus or promotional points?**
   We run promotions with bonus points occasionally. When we do, we'll let you know. Promotional points follow the same expiry and forfeiture rules unless we say otherwise.

8. **When do my points show up?**
   Points are added to your Reward Points balance when the transaction **settles** or the criteria of the eligible activity is met, not when you authorise it. They may appear as pending briefly. You can access the Reward Points balance in the Rewards section in the Prospa app and Customer Portal. For Qantas Points conversions, once the conversion succeeds, Qantas Points appear in your Qantas Business Rewards account straight away.

9. **Do points expire?**
   Yes. If there's **no new points earned for 18 months**, your points expire and we can't reinstate them. We may also forfeit points if your account closes or our terms are breached.

10. **Can I get cash for my points?**
    No — Reward Points can't be exchanged for cash. You can convert them to Qantas Points or redeem them for gift cards and other rewards.

**Can I transfer points to someone else?**
Prospa Reward Points are non‑transferable and stay linked to your Prospa account and ABN. However, once you convert Reward Points to Qantas Points via Qantas Business Rewards, you can use your Qantas account settings to allocate or use those Qantas Points for others (e.g. employees or family) — for example, to book travel or purchase gift cards. Qantas terms apply.

11. **What happens to my points if I close my Prospa account?**
    Any unused points are forfeited when your Prospa Account is closed. We don't pay out or replace them.

## Convert your Reward Points to Qantas Points

1. **What is Qantas Business Rewards?**
   Qantas Business Rewards is a loyalty program for Australian small and medium businesses. You can earn Qantas Points on business expenses and use them for flights, upgrades and other benefits. Points earned by your business are stored in your Qantas Business Rewards membership account. Visit [Qantas Business Rewards](https://www.qantas.com/au/en/frequent-flyer/qantas-business-rewards.html) for the full range of benefits.

2. **Do I need a Qantas account to earn Reward Points?**
   No. You don't need Qantas Business Rewards to earn Reward Points with Prospa. You only need a Qantas Business Rewards membership account if you want to **convert** your Reward Points into Qantas Points.

3. **What is the conversion rate of Reward Points to Qantas Points?**
   **2 Reward Points = 1 Qantas Point.** For example, 2,000 Reward Points = 1,000 Qantas Points. You convert in whole increments of 2 Reward Points.

4. **What is the minimum Reward Point amount to convert to Qantas Points?**
   You need at least **2,000 Reward Points** to convert. You convert in **increments of 2 Reward Points** (2 Reward Points = 1 Qantas Point).

5. **How do I convert Reward Points to Qantas Points?**
   You can convert **Reward Points to Qantas Points** in the Prospa App or customer portal. You'll need a Qantas Business Rewards account — you can join free through the Prospa App or customer portal if you're not already a member. Choose how many points to convert (2 Reward Points = 1 Qantas Point). After a successful conversion, Qantas Points will appear in your Qantas Business Rewards account.

6. **How do I join Qantas Business Rewards?**
   You can join for free through the Prospa App or customer portal. Your ABN becomes your QBR membership number. Qantas' terms apply once you're a member.

7. **How do I activate my Qantas Business Rewards account?**
   Once you've signed up via Prospa, you'll receive an activation email from Qantas Business Rewards. Use the unique link in that email to set up your account. If that doesn't work, contact the Qantas Business Rewards Service Centre on **13 74 78** (Monday–Friday) or contact them online.

8. **I converted Reward Points into Qantas Points but I can't see them in my Qantas Business Rewards account?**
   If the conversion completed in our system, Qantas Points are credited to your connected Qantas Business Rewards account within 24 hours. Check your Qantas Business Rewards account or contact the Qantas Business Rewards Service Centre on **13 74 78**.

## Support

1. **Where is my payment confirmation?**
   You can track your payment status in the Prospa App or customer portal under your Pay By Card transaction history. We'll send you a confirmation once your payment has settled.

2. **Why can't I add my biller?**
   Check that the BSB, account number, account name and reference are correct. Some billers need extra verification (for example, an industry code). If it fails, update the details and try again — the message in the app or portal will guide you.

3. **What if my card expired or was replaced?**
   Update or add a new card in Pay By Card settings in the Prospa App or customer portal. Old or expired cards can cause your payments to fail.

4. **Who do I contact for payment or fee errors?**
   Contact Prospa support via the Prospa App or Customer Portal and include your payment reference. We'll track it down and let you know what happens next.

5. **Who do I contact for points or conversion issues?**
   For missing or incorrect points, or if a conversion didn't complete, contact Prospa support. For Qantas Points already in your Qantas Business Rewards account, contact the Qantas Business Rewards Service Centre on **13 74 78**.

6. **Why were my points reversed?**
   This is usually because of a refund, chargeback or ineligible transaction. We may also adjust for errors or misuse. We don't compensate for reversed or forfeited points.

7. **What if I have a refund on a payment I earned points on?**
   We may reverse or reduce the points. The Reward fee isn't refunded for the points portion unless our terms say otherwise.

8. **Why did my payment fail?**
   Your card or issuer may have declined the payment (for example, due to a limit, insufficient funds or a fraud check). No fee is charged on failed payments. You can try another card or contact your card issuer.

9. **The biller didn't receive the payment.**
   Check the payment status in the Prospa App or customer portal.

   - **Paid** means we've sent the transfer — allow time for the bank to process it.
   - **Processing** means we're still processing it (allow up to 2 business days).
   - **Declined** means no transfer was made. We'll explain any refunds if that applies.

10. **I was charged twice.**
    Contact Prospa support with your payment reference(s). We'll check for duplicates and follow our refund process if needed.

11. **Can I get a refund?**
    Contact Prospa support with your payment details and we'll walk you through it. Keep in mind that any Reward Points earned on that payment may be reversed as part of the process.

12. **Why are my points "pending"?**
    Points can stay pending until the transaction settles and we've finished verification. After that they're available. For Qantas conversions, points land in your Qantas Business Rewards account usually within 24 hours.
