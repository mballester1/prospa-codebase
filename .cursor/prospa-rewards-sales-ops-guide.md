# Prospa Rewards – Sales Agent Guide

**For:** Sales Ops team | **Use when:** Inbound calls or enquiries about the Prospa Rewards loyalty program

---

## Why We're Doing This

### The Problem

Running a small business means managing cash flow, admin and bills. Today, many customers:

* Can't earn rewards on key spend because some billers (e.g. ATO, certain suppliers) don't accept cards.
* Lose interest‑free days when they're forced to pay by bank transfer.
* Waste time across banks, portals and spreadsheets just to track what's paid.

Prospa Rewards, built on our payments products (starting with Pay By Card and Business Account), changes that.

### With Pay By Card, customers can:

* Pay almost any biller by card (even if the biller doesn't take cards) while we pay the biller via bank transfer.
* Use card credit terms to smooth cash flow and still pay suppliers/ATO on time.
* Manage bills in one place and reduce manual admin.

### With Prospa Rewards enabled:

* Earn Reward Points on eligible spend.
* "Double dip" by keeping their card rewards and earning Prospa Reward Points for a small reward fee when they opt in.
* Convert points to Qantas Points (and later other options like gift cards), turning everyday bills into tangible value.

### For Prospa, this:

* Increases payments volume by making "Pay and Earn" the default.
* Builds engagement and stickiness through ongoing usage.
* Differentiates us from providers that don't support card‑to‑bank bill payments or meaningful business rewards.

**In short:** Prospa Rewards makes bill paying simpler, supports cash flow, and gives customers value back on spend they already must make.

## What's in for our customers?

* Prospa Rewards lets customers **earn Reward Points** on eligible activity (e.g. Pay By Card)
* **Earn:** 1 Reward Point per $1 of eligible spend (e.g. Pay By Card); a **1% + GST reward fee** applies when they opt in to earn on a particular payment.
* **Double dip** with Pay By Card: they keep their card's own points and earn Prospa Reward Points for an extra 1% reward fee when they opt in.
* **Redeem:**
    * Convert your points to Qantas Points (**2 Reward Points = 1 Qantas Point)** via the Prospa and or Customer Portal. No redemption fee.
    * **Join Qantas Business Rewards for free or link their existing Qantas mebership account** in the Prospa App or customer portal if they want to convert to Qantas Points.

On day 1, customers earn Reward Points only via Pay By Card and can redeem them only for Qantas points. We will add more earning and redemption options in the coming months.

Customers can only earn Qantas points directly with a [**Prospa Small Business Loan or Business Loan Plus**](https://prospa.atlassian.net/wiki/x/IwBW-w)

## Program Overview

| Aspect | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Earning Reward Points** | Customers with a Prospa Account earn **Reward Points** on eligible activity.  The first product that earns points at launch is **Pay By Card**: **1 Reward Point per full $1** of eligible transaction value (rounded down; GST excluded).  They must opt in to earn points on each Pay By Card payment (in-app toggle). |
| **Reward fee** | When Prospa Rewards are enabled on a payment, a **reward fee of 1% + GST** generally applies. The customer sees the fee and earning potential before confirming. |
| **Redemption** | Minimum **2,000 Reward Points** to redeem.  Redemption in **increments of 2 Reward Points**. **2 Reward Points = 1 Qantas Point.** No service fee for redeeming. |
| **Qantas** | Redemption to Qantas requires an eligible **Qantas Business Rewards (QBR)** membership.  Customers can join QBR for free through the Prospa App or customer portal.  After successful redemption, points are converted and appear in the customer's QBR account **within two business days**. |
| **Expiry & forfeiture** | Points expire after **18 months** of no qualifying earning activity. Points are forfeited on account closure, ABN deregistration, insolvency, breach of terms, or misuse. |

## FAQs for Sales Agents

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| **What is Prospa Rewards?** | Our loyalty program. Customers earn Reward Points on eligible payments or activity (e.g. Pay By Card) and can convert them to Qantas Points or other rewards (e.g. gift cards when available) in the Prospa App or customer portal. |
| **How do customers earn Reward Points?** | Via Pay By Card: turn on Prospa Rewards for each payment and earn **1 Reward Point per $1** of eligible spend. A **reward fee of 1% + GST** applies when they opt in. More ways to earn may be added later. |
| **Do they earn on every payment?** | No. Only eligible Pay By Card transactions **with rewards turned on** earn points. Prospa is working to add more earn options in the coming months. |
| **Where can I find my Reward Points balance?** | Customers can view their points balance in the Rewards section of the App and Portal. |
| **What's the minimum number of Reward Points I can redeem?** | **2,000 Reward Points.** Redemption is in increments of 2 Reward Points (2 Reward Points = 1 Qantas Point). |
| **Do they need a Qantas account to earn Reward Points?** | No. A Qantas Business Rewards membership is only needed if they want to **convert** Reward Points to Qantas Points. |
| **How do they convert to Qantas Points?** | In the Prospa App or customer portal. They need a Qantas Business Rewards account (can join for free via Prospa). After a successful conversion, Qantas Points appear in their QBR account within **2 business days**. |
| **Do points expire?** | Yes. If no new Reward Points are earned for **18 months**, points expire and can't be reinstated. Points are also forfeited on account closure or breach of terms. |
| **Can they get cash for points?** | No. Reward Points can't be exchanged for cash. They can be converted to Qantas Points or redeemed for other options when available. |
| **What if they close their Prospa account?** | All unused Reward Points are forfeited. Advise them to convert any points to Qantas (or other options) in the app or portal **before** closing the account. |
