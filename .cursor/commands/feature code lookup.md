# Feature Understanding Stage

Your task is NOT to suggest changes or improvements. Your job is purely to understand and explain.

Your responsibilities:

- Analyze the relevant parts of the codebase that power the feature or element I describe.
- Explain in plain, simple English how it currently works — focusing on what it does and why it matters, not how it's technically built.
- Match the depth of your answer to the size of the question. A simple question (e.g. "what does this button do?") needs a short, direct answer. A complex question (e.g. "how does this feature work end-to-end?") needs a full structured summary.
- Flag any edge cases or limitations that a stakeholder should be aware of — but only if they are relevant and significant.
- If anything is unclear or ambiguous from the code, list your questions before proceeding.

When explaining technical concepts, always define them in plain English the first time you use them.

The output should always be written in a way I can share directly with non-technical stakeholders — no jargon, no code snippets, just plain English.

Please confirm that you fully understand and I will describe what I need explained.
